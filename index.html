The itinerary

Bangkok → Phuket (with a Koh Yao Yai day trip) → Krabi · Sat 1 – Sat 8 May 2027

Bangkok · Day 1 · Sat 1 May

Arrive, regroup, Reclining Buddha

  • Flights land through the day; check in on Sukhumvit (Soi 11–21), rest, pool time
  • Late afternoon: Wat Pho and the 46 m Reclining Buddha
  • Reunion dinner at an Indian restaurant on Sukhumvit
  • After dinner: taxi (~30 min) to Asiatique for the Ferris wheel and river views
🥗Veg: Bawarchi (Soi 21), Dosa King, Saras; Punjab Sweets in Pahurat for pure-veg thali
Bangkok · Day 2 · Sun 2 May

Grand Palace, sharks, night market

  • 8:30 AM: Grand Palace & Wat Phra Kaew at opening, before the heat. Cover shoulders and knees
  • Late morning: SEA LIFE Ocean World under Siam Paragon (air-conditioned)
  • Afternoon: naps and the hotel pool
  • Evening: Jodd Fairs night market or a relaxed Sukhumvit dinner. Pack for Phuket
✈️ Day 3 morning · Bangkok → Phuket · ~1.5 hr
Phuket · Day 3 · Mon 3 May

Fly south, first beach afternoon

  • Morning flight to Phuket (HKT); two vans to Kata (~1 hr), check in by lunch
  • Afternoon: Kata Noi beach, soft sand and shallow water for under-8s
  • Late afternoon: resort pool and kids' club
🥗Veg dinner: Tandoori Flames (Kata). Resort kitchens make dal, paneer and roti on request
Phuket · Day 4 · Tue 4 May

Koh Yao Yai day trip

  • 8:30 AM van to Ao Po Grand Marina (~50 min), speedboat ~20 min to Koh Yao Yai
  • Resort beach day: ask Santhiya or Elixir about a day pass with lunch and boats
  • Walk out on the Laem Had sandbar at low tide for the group photo
  • Optional: a private speedboat that adds James Bond Island (Phang Nga Bay)
  • Under-4s: a 15-min longtail to Coral Island from Chalong Pier instead
🥗Veg: pre-order lunch with the resort and carry snacks. The island has few shops
Phuket · Day 5 · Wed 5 May

Water park and Old Town

  • Morning: Splash Jungle: wave pool, lazy river, shallow kids' zone
  • Lunch and an afternoon stroll in Old Phuket Town (Soi Rommanee)
  • Pool, naps, pack for Krabi
  • Optional evening: Phuket FantaSea show + buffet with a veg section
🚐 Day 6 morning · Phuket → Ao Nang · 2.5–3.5 hr by van
Krabi · Day 6 · Thu 6 May

Over to Krabi's limestone cliffs

  • 9 AM: two private vans from Kata to Ao Nang, arriving for lunch
  • Afternoon: pool and naps
  • Sunset: walk the Ao Nang beachfront as the longtails come in
  • Optional for older kids: mangrove kayaking at Ao Thalane
🥗Veg: Govinda's and Little Govinda's (pure veg), Pure Vegetarian Dosa Corner, Pakwan
Krabi · Day 7 · Fri 7 May

Railay and the 4-island longtail loop

  • Two chartered longtails: Phra Nang Cave Beach, Tup Island sandbar, Chicken Island, Poda Island
  • Life jackets for every child; ask for a boat with a canopy
  • Under-4s: a short hop to Railay and back by lunch
  • Evening: farewell dinner, one long table for 19
Krabi · Day 8 · Sat 8 May

Last swim, flights home

  • Breakfast, last swim, checkout
  • Krabi Airport (KBV) is ~30–40 min away. Check IndiGo's direct flights to BOM, BLR and DEL; Hyderabad connects via Bangkok
  • No suitable KBV flight? Van back to Phuket airport (~2.5–3.5 hr)

Tips

Food, kids, boats and the small things that make a group trip smooth

🥗 Veg food, city by city

  • Bangkok: Sukhumvit Soi 11–21 has 20+ Indian places: Bawarchi, Dosa King, Saras. Punjab Sweets in Pahurat
  • Phuket: Tandoori Flames (Kata), Kinnaree, Royal Indian. Resorts make dal and roti on request
  • Koh Yao Yai: pre-order lunch and carry snacks
  • Krabi: Govinda's, Little Govinda's, Dosa Corner, Pakwan

🌊 Boats in May

The Andaman monsoon starts around now, so some days are choppy. Keep Day 4 and Day 7 swappable with a beach day, book tours that refund for weather, and swim only between the lifeguard flags.

👶 With under-8s

  • Plan a nap or pool break every afternoon in 32 °C heat
  • Child life jackets on every boat; ask for canopies
  • Water shoes for wet longtail landings
  • Diapers and ORS are cheap at 7-Eleven and pharmacies

🚐 Getting around as 19

  • Two 10-seat vans for every transfer, with luggage space
  • Phuket → Ao Nang by road takes 2.5–3.5 hr. The ferry via Phi Phi takes 4–4.5 hr and is harder with bags
  • Koh Yao Yai speedboats leave from Ao Po Grand Marina

💵 Money

ATMs charge about 220 THB per withdrawal, so take out larger amounts. SuperRich in Bangkok has good exchange rates. Longtails, park fees and Koh Yao Yai are mostly cash-only.

🛂 Entry

Indian passport holders currently get visa-free entry. Check the allowed stay and the Thailand Digital Arrival Card requirement shortly before you fly; both rules have changed recently.

🎒 Pack

Reef-safe SPF 50+, DEET repellent, light cotton, a compact umbrella, dry bags, water shoes, a universal adapter (Type A/B/C, 220 V), and modest clothes for temples.

  • By Feb 2027: hotels in Bangkok, Phuket, Ao Nang

  • By Apr 2027: KYY day trip, vans, Krabi island tour
